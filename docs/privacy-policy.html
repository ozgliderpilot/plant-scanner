Privacy Policy — GF Nursery

Last updated: 4 August 2026

GF Nursery (“the app”) is a volunteer plant-nursery tool. It helps staff scan plant labels and record sales, culls, label prints, and repots. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

Who this is for

The app is intended for authorised nursery volunteers and operators. It is not a consumer social app and is not aimed at children.

Camera

The app requests camera permission to scan Code 128 barcodes on plant labels. The camera is used for barcode recognition only. The app does not store or upload photos as a gallery or camera roll feature.

Data the app processes

On the device

  • Connection settings you enter (sync URL, access code, device prefix)
  • Local records of sales, culls, label print requests, and repots (including scanned accession codes, quantities, amounts you enter, and related plant details from the nursery list)
  • A local copy of the plant list used for offline lookup

When you sync

Those pending records and plant-list updates are sent to the nursery’s own Google Apps Script / Google Sheet backend that operators configure in Settings. The app does not send this data to us as a separate commercial service, advertising network, or analytics vendor.

What we do not do

  • No advertising in the app
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app as shipped
  • No user accounts or social login (access is via the nursery’s configured sync URL and access code)

Who can see synced data

People who have access to the nursery’s Google Sheet / Apps Script deployment (typically nursery operators) can see synced operational records. Device settings (including the access code) stay on the device unless someone with the device opens Settings.

Data retention

Local data remains on the device until cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app data. Retention of data in the Google Sheet is controlled by the nursery operators who manage that Sheet.

Children’s privacy

The app is a workplace/volunteer tool for nursery operations, not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy for that use.

Contact

For privacy questions about GF Nursery, contact the nursery operator who provided the app or who manages the nursery Google Sheet used for sync.
